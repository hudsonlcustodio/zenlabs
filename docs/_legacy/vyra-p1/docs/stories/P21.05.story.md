## User story

COMO cliente, QUERO ver todo o conteúdo em produção agrupado por onde ele está, PARA que o que espera por mim seja impossível de perder e o que falhou não fique escondido.

## Context

`FR-P03` asks to track in-flight content and its state. `VYRA_PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE.md` §26.2 collapses 21 states into five lanes and makes a deliberate choice: `FAILED` and `BLOCKED` get **no lane**, because hiding problems in a problems column is how problems get ignored. §21.3 requires every `BLOCKED` reason to carry its next action.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Two views over one dataset — pipeline board (`L-board`) and table (`L-collection`) — with the view choice persisted per user and reflected in the URL (§26.1).
- **AC-2** The five lanes of §26.2 render with the exact state grouping given there, `Your review` first, and lane counts reflecting the **server total** rather than the loaded page.
- **AC-3** `FAILED` and `BLOCKED` items render **in place** with a danger treatment; a filter chip in the context bar shows their count. Neither is moved to a separate lane or tab.
- **AC-4** Every `BLOCKED` item renders its reason from the closed set of §21.3 with its next action. `ingestion_failed` states that VYRA is recovering the item **and that it will not be re-billed**, per `ADR-0034` and `FF-32`, and exposes no retry or re-render control.
- **AC-5** Drag is disabled on the Portal board: the client's only pipeline write is scheduling from `READY`, which happens in the calendar or the item drawer. A non-draggable card states this on grab attempt (`P19.08` AC-5).
- **AC-6** The item drawer of §31.6 renders the state timeline, the current required action, and per-tab `absent-by-design` states for a video, publication or performance that does not exist yet. Below `md` it is a bottom sheet.

## BDD examples

- EX-P21-03 GIVEN an item in `BLOCKED` with reason `ingestion_failed`, WHEN it renders, THEN the Portal states VYRA is recovering it and that it will not be re-billed.
- EX-P21-20 GIVEN a `FAILED` item, WHEN the board renders, THEN it appears in its lane with a danger treatment and is counted in the problems chip.
- EX-P21-21 GIVEN a `BLOCKED` item with reason `ingestion_failed`, WHEN the drawer renders, THEN no retry or re-render control exists.

## Verification gate

Board and table views round-trip through the URL; five lanes match §26.2 with server-total counts; failed and blocked render in place with a problems filter chip; every blocked reason renders with its next action; `ingestion_failed` states recovery and non-re-billing and exposes no re-render; Portal drag is disabled with a stated reason; the item drawer renders the timeline and per-tab absent states.
