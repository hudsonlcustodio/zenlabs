# Story P23.07 — Cost and contribution with estimated-value distinction

## User story

COMO administrador, QUERO distinguir custo reportado de custo estimado em toda visão, PARA que nenhuma decisão comercial seja tomada sobre um número que parecia um fato.

## Context

`FR-C05` covers provider cost ledgers and derived contribution. `RISK-14` and `GATE-COST01` record that not every provider exposes a per-job cost, and `provider-cost-ledger.md` requires entries to be marked `estimated` and never invented. `ADR-0025` keeps the provider cost ledger separate from the client consumption ledger. `VYRA_PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE.md` §29.4 is explicit: an estimate is never presented as a fact.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** The screen renders per §33.6: a KPI row (revenue proxy, provider cost, contribution), a contribution chart using the diverging palette, a cost grid and an entry drawer.
- **AC-2** **Estimated and reported costs are visually distinct in every view** — a dashed underline plus an `estimated` chip on values, and a distinct treatment in the chart. A seeded unmarked estimate fails the assertion.
- **AC-3** A set mixing estimated and reported values **never aggregates into a single unqualified number**: the aggregate is labelled and states how much of it is estimated.
- **AC-4** A provider that reports no cost field at all is stated as such per provider rather than rendering zero (`GATE-COST01`, `RISK-14`).
- **AC-5** The two ledgers are never conflated: client entitlement consumption and VYRA provider cost appear as separate concepts with separate units, per `ADR-0025` and `FR-PC01`. A view combining them into one figure fails the review checklist.
- **AC-6** The cost ledger is append-only with the same affordance rules as `P23.06` AC-2, and export is available with the estimated/reported distinction preserved in the exported data.

## BDD examples

- EX-P23-02 GIVEN a provider that does not report per-job cost, WHEN its entries render, THEN they are marked `estimated` and are never aggregated into an unqualified total.
- EX-P23-19 GIVEN an export of mixed entries, WHEN it is produced, THEN the estimated/reported distinction is preserved in the exported data.
- EX-P23-20 GIVEN a provider with no cost field, WHEN its row renders, THEN it states the absence rather than showing zero.

## Verification gate

KPI row, diverging contribution chart, cost grid and entry drawer render; the estimated/reported distinction is present in every view and the assertion turns red on a seeded unmarked estimate; mixed aggregates are labelled with their estimated share; a provider without a cost field states the absence; the two ledgers remain separate concepts; export preserves the distinction.
