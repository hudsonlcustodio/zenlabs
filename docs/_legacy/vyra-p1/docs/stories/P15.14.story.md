## User story

COMO QA reviewer, QUERO a queue of items awaiting my verdict and a place to record pass or fail with findings, PARA the quality gate is worked rather than assumed.

## Context

This is the Studio QA screen that `epic-P12` and `P12.02` place out of their own scope. FR-S06 requires performing QA and recording outcomes. `api-contracts.md` §4 fixes `POST /studio/content-items/{id}/qa` as `QA_REVIEWER`-only with a mandatory human verdict, mapping to T13 and T14.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** The queue renders oldest-item age, which `observability.md` §4 alarms on as RISK-18 relief, so a growing backlog is visible to the reviewer before it is visible to a client.
- **AC-2** The verdict control posts `verdict` and optional `findings` exactly as the §4 body specifies; no system actor path exists from this screen.
- **AC-3** A failing verdict cannot be submitted without findings that satisfy the T14 precondition.
- **AC-4** The screen never exposes a control that would let a tenant approval policy stand in for a verdict; FF-33 remains true regardless of what is rendered.

Class 6 coverage asserting only `QA_REVIEWER` may submit from this screen; class 5 coverage of T13 and T14 unaffected by screen state.
