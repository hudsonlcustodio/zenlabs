# Story P18.06 — Motion system and the reduced-motion contract

- **Story ID**: `P18.06`

## User story

COMO frontend engineer, QUERO durações, easings e um vocabulário de movimento com contrato de reduced-motion, PARA que movimento explique estado e nunca entretenha, e que a preferência do usuário seja estrutural.

## Context

`VYRA_PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE.md` §10 fixes six durations, four easings, two animatable properties and eight motion patterns. PD-06 is the principle; §10.4 is the contract. Motion for React is sanctioned by ADR-0035 for orchestration and gesture, but this story builds the token and rule layer both engines obey.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** The six duration tokens and four easing tokens of §10.1 and §10.2 exist, and enter/exit/move/drop each map to their prescribed easing. No duration exceeds 480ms except an indeterminate progress indicator.
- **AC-2** Only `transform` and `opacity` are animatable. A transition or animation on `height`, `width`, `top`, `left`, `box-shadow`, `filter` or `background-position` fails the motion lint naming the property; height change uses the sanctioned grid-template-rows or scale technique.
- **AC-3** The eight motion patterns of §10.5 exist as reusable primitives: reveal, drawer, dialog, state change, value change, live, drag and list reflow. List reflow is capped at 24 animated rows and disables beyond that.
- **AC-4** The reduced-motion contract of §10.4 is implemented globally: `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` sets every duration to 1ms except opacity cross-fades at `dur-fast`; `glow-live` becomes static; skeleton shimmer becomes a flat block; chart draw-in is skipped; drag keeps its keyboard equivalent with no fly animation.
- **AC-5** Nothing auto-advances anywhere in the product, reduced motion or not, and a lint rule rejects an infinite animation outside the two sanctioned live indicators.
- **AC-6** A **reduced-motion test** enumerates every animated primitive under the reduce preference and asserts no computed duration exceeds 1ms except the sanctioned fades.

## BDD examples

- EX-P18-03 GIVEN `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, WHEN any primitive animates, THEN no duration exceeds 1ms except a sanctioned opacity fade.
- EX-P18-19 GIVEN a component transitioning `height`, WHEN the motion lint runs, THEN it fails naming the property.

## Verification gate

Duration and easing tokens exported; the motion lint turns red on a seeded non-transform/opacity transition and on a seeded infinite animation; the eight patterns render correctly; the reduced-motion test passes for every animated primitive.
