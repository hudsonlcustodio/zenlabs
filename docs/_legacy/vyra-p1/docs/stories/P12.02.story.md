## User story

COMO QA reviewer, QUERO routes to pass or fail an item with a reason, PARA the QA gate is operable by the people accountable for it.

## Context

`workflows-state-machines.md` T13 and T14; `api-contracts.md` §4 places these among internal Studio resources.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** T13 and T14 are exposed as routes restricted to `QA_REVIEWER` by the P3.06 guards.
- **AC-2** T14 refuses a submission without a reason, per its precondition.
- **AC-3** Routes validate request and response against Zod schemas (class 3).
- **AC-4** Both routes are idempotent under the keys `attemptId+qa` and `attemptId+qafail`.
