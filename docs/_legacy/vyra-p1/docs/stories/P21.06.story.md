## User story

COMO cliente, QUERO assistir e recuperar meus vídeos com uma experiência de reprodução real, PARA que o produto final pareça o produto final e não um anexo.

## Context

`FR-P07` asks for a library of approved and published assets. `security-architecture.md` §6 makes short-TTL signed URLs the only delivery path and `EX-P15-05` asserts it. `FF-15` makes AI disclosure non-suppressible. `VYRA_PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE.md` §24 and §31.10 fix the presentation.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** The library renders a responsive media grid — 4/3/2/1 columns — with tiles carrying poster, duration, channel mark, AI disclosure and status, and skeletons that preserve aspect ratio while loading.
- **AC-2** Every playback uses the `P19.05` player with a signed URL obtained from the API; a bucket or public URL is never constructed and never rendered (`EX-P15-05`).
- **AC-3** A signed URL expiring during playback refreshes transparently; a refresh failure surfaces as a tile- or player-scoped error with retry, never a silent stall.
- **AC-4** AI disclosure appears on the player **and** on every card representing the asset, and is not removable by any prop or tenant setting (`FF-15`).
- **AC-5** Missing posters render a `bg-sunken` frame carrying the content title — never a spinner over black (§24.2).
- **AC-6** Below `md`, tiles are 16:9 full-width with the title beneath, playback opens the sheet player, and every player control meets the 44×44 coarse-pointer minimum.
- **AC-7** Archived assets render dimmed with an `Archived` chip and remain playable; filtered-empty and first-run empty states are distinct (§19.1).

## BDD examples

- EX-P15-05 GIVEN a media asset in the library, WHEN it is viewed, THEN delivery uses a short-TTL signed URL and never a public object.
- EX-P21-22 GIVEN an asset without a poster, WHEN its tile renders, THEN a titled sunken frame is shown rather than a spinner.
- EX-P21-23 GIVEN any caller in the Portal, WHEN it attempts to hide the AI disclosure, THEN no such capability exists.

## Verification gate

Grid and tile geometry render at all breakpoints with aspect-preserving skeletons; playback works only from signed URLs and transparent refresh is asserted; disclosure is present on player and cards and is unremovable; poster fallback renders titled; mobile sheet playback and 44×44 controls asserted; archived, filtered-empty and first-run states are distinct.
