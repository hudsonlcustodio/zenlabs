## User story

COMO cliente, QUERO ver e agendar publicações num calendário com fuso explícito, PARA que eu nunca publique no horário errado nem descubra uma restrição de canal depois do fato.

## Context

`FR-P06` asks for the editorial calendar and T17 is the scheduling transition. `VYRA_PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE.md` §27 fixes the views, the timezone rule and the scheduling interaction; §26.3 fixes the drag semantics — intent expressed, engine decides (`FF-06`). `GATE-TT01` means a TikTok item may only publish `SELF_ONLY`, and §27.4 requires that to be stated **before** scheduling.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Month, week and agenda views render per §27.1 with view and date range in the URL; month at `≥ lg`, week at `md`, agenda below `md`.
- **AC-2** The calendar header declares its timezone; where the viewer's timezone differs from the tenant's, both are shown on hover and in the schedule drawer. No publication time renders without a timezone anywhere (`P19.09` AC-3).
- **AC-3** Drag-to-schedule and drag-to-reschedule are enabled at `≥ md` with valid targets supplied as data: only `READY` items may be scheduled, only future dates accepted, and a `SCHEDULED` item may move until `PUBLISHING` begins. Each drag has the full keyboard equivalent from `P19.08` AC-3.
- **AC-4** A drop the server refuses reverts with `emphasized` easing and raises a **region-level** error naming the reason — never a toast (§26.3).
- **AC-5** Conflict detection warns when two publications target the same channel within the configured minimum interval (`OQ-PX-03`, `ASM-P21-03`); it warns and does not block, unless the platform itself rejects it.
- **AC-6** A TikTok item under `GATE-TT01` renders a `restricted` badge and states that the post will be `SELF_ONLY` **before** scheduling; the unscheduled rail lists `READY` items sorted by age and collapses to a counted chip at `md`.

## BDD examples

- EX-P21-08 GIVEN a scheduled TikTok item under `GATE-TT01`, WHEN it renders on the calendar, THEN the `SELF_ONLY` restriction is stated before scheduling, not after publication.
- EX-P21-24 GIVEN a drag onto a past date, WHEN the target is evaluated, THEN it is skipped as invalid and announced.
- EX-P21-25 GIVEN two publications to one channel inside the minimum interval, WHEN they render, THEN both carry a warning ring and the drawer explains it.

## Verification gate

Three views round-trip through the URL; timezone is always present and dual-displayed when it differs; drag and its keyboard equivalent schedule and reschedule only valid targets; a refused drop reverts with a region error; conflict warnings appear without blocking; `GATE-TT01` restriction is stated before scheduling; the unscheduled rail sorts by age and collapses at `md`.
