## User story

COMO frontend engineer, QUERO raio, borda e elevação como tokens com uma única espessura de borda e sombra apenas para o que flutua, PARA que profundidade venha de superfície e não de sombra em conteúdo plano.

## Context

`VYRA_PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE.md` §9 fixes seven radius steps, one border weight, five elevation levels and four sanctioned glows. PD-04 states the product principle behind it: a shadow on flat content is the fastest way to look like a template.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** The seven radius tokens of §9.1 exist, and the concentric-nesting rule — inner radius = outer radius − padding, floored at `radius-sm` — is implemented by the surface primitives rather than repeated by hand.
- **AC-2** Exactly one border weight (1px) is expressible. The two 2px exceptions — the active rail indicator and the focus ring — are named indicator tokens, not border tokens, and a 2px border in any component fails the lint.
- **AC-3** The five elevation levels of §9.3 exist as composable treatments. `e0`–`e2` carry **no shadow**; a shadow applied to a card, table, chart or input fails the elevation lint naming the component.
- **AC-4** The four sanctioned glows of §9.4 exist and no fifth is expressible. `glow-live` is capped at one instance per viewport region by the component API, and degrades to a static ring under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **AC-5** Backdrop blur is available at exactly two call sites — the modal scrim and the media control scrim — capped at 8px, and a blur applied elsewhere fails the lint (§9.5).

## BDD examples

- EX-P18-17 GIVEN a card with a box-shadow, WHEN the elevation lint runs, THEN it fails naming the component.
- EX-P18-18 GIVEN two `glow-live` indicators in one region, WHEN the component renders, THEN the second is refused and the violation is reported in development.

## Verification gate

Radius, border, elevation and glow tokens exported; the elevation lint turns red on a seeded card shadow, a seeded 2px border and a seeded third blur site; the concentric-nesting rule is asserted by a computed-style test on nested surfaces.
