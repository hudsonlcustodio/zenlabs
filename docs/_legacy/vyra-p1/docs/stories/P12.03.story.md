## User story

COMO portal user, QUERO to approve or reject a script and a video with a reason, PARA I control what goes out in my name.

## Context

FR-P04 and FR-P05 require approval with a reason on rejection. T05, T06, T15 and T16 fix the transitions; `api-contracts.md` §3.2 places them on the Portal surface.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Script approve/reject and video approve/reject are exposed as Portal routes restricted to the portal user.
- **AC-2** Rejection requires a reason, per the T06 and T16 preconditions.
- **AC-3** Each route is idempotent under its declared key from the transition table.
- **AC-4** T15 cannot succeed without a passing QA record, regardless of tenant policy.

## BDD examples

- EX-P12-01 GIVEN a tenant with AUTO video approval, WHEN no human QA verdict exists, THEN the item cannot reach READY.
