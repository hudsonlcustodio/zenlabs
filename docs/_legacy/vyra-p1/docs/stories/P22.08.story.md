## User story

COMO publicador, QUERO um calendário que atravesse clientes sem confundi-los, PARA que eu planeje a operação inteira sem perder de vista de quem é cada publicação.

## Context

`FR-S08` covers calendar management across clients. `VYRA_PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE.md` §32.12 extends the Portal calendar with a client dimension: chips are client-coloured only when unscoped, the unscheduled rail groups by client, and conflict rules apply per client per channel rather than globally.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Month and week views render at `≥ lg` and week only at `md`, over the `P19.09` primitives, with view and range in the URL.
- **AC-2** When unscoped, chips carry a client identifier and the unscheduled rail groups by client; when scoped, the client dimension disappears entirely rather than becoming a redundant repeated label.
- **AC-3** Conflict detection applies per client per channel: two publications to the same channel for **different** clients is not a conflict; for the same client within the configured interval it warns without blocking (`ASM-P21-03`).
- **AC-4** Drag-to-schedule and reschedule use `P19.08` with validity supplied as data and the full keyboard equivalent; a refused drop reverts with a region-level error.
- **AC-5** Bulk scheduling over selected unscheduled items is available and states how many items and which clients it will affect before executing.

## BDD examples

- EX-P22-21 GIVEN two publications to one channel for different clients, WHEN conflict detection runs, THEN no conflict is raised.
- EX-P22-22 GIVEN a bulk schedule of six items across two clients, WHEN it is confirmed, THEN the counts and clients were stated beforehand.

## Verification gate

Month and week render at their breakpoints and round-trip through the URL; the client dimension appears only when unscoped; conflict rules are per client per channel; drag and its keyboard equivalent work with data-supplied validity and revert on refusal; bulk scheduling states scope before executing.
