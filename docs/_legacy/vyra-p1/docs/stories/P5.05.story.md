## User story

COMO identity owner, QUERO to complete provider verification personally, PARA the ethical safeguard on voice cloning is respected rather than routed around.

## Context

`workflows-state-machines.md` §4 and `prd.md` §9.2. PVC permits cloning only your own voice and requires verification; FR-VC01 requires the architecture to let the owner complete it personally. FR-ID06 forbids designing any bypass.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** The states and transitions of `workflows-state-machines.md` §4 are implemented exactly.
- **AC-2** Both IVC and PVC paths are representable, with their differing sample and verification requirements (OQ-P5-01).
- **AC-3** A clone reaches ready only on a provider-returned verification success; no local flag can substitute.
- **AC-4** The flow allows the identity owner personally to complete verification (FR-VC01).
- **AC-5** GATE-EL01 is recorded on the PVC path; the mock stands in until workspace requirements are confirmed.
