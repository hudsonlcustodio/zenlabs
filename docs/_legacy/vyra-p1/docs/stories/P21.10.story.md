## User story

COMO cliente, QUERO entender meu plano, meu consumo, o estado do meu gêmeo digital e meu consentimento em superfícies que digam a verdade sobre o que é reversível, PARA que governança seja visível e não implícita.

## Context

This story delivers the experience for the four concerns `P15.11` carries — plan and payment (`FR-P09`, `FR-P10`), consumption as a ledger fold (`FR-UC05`, `FF-07`), Digital Twin state (`FR-P11`) and consent capture (`FR-ID02`, `FR-ID03`). The audit recorded that composite as an accident of authoring order; separating the **experience** into distinct surfaces here resolves it without renumbering `P15`. `VYRA_PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE.md` §30.2 and §30.3 fix twin state and consent.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Plan and consumption render per §31.12: a hero consumption meter, period selector, per-item breakdown and ledger history. Reserved capacity is shown **separately** from committed, because a reservation is not a charge, and every number folds from the ledger (`FF-07`).
- **AC-2** Threshold states are distinct: healthy, approaching limit at a configurable threshold, and exhausted — the last stating plainly that new requests and approvals are blocked and linking to the surfaces that say so.
- **AC-3** Subscription and payment render per §31.13 with `active`, `past_due`, `suspended` and `cancelled`; `ADR-0029` leaves payment an extension point, so the UI states the absence of self-serve payment as deliberate and never as "coming soon" (NG6).
- **AC-4** The Twin presence module renders the five states of §30.2 with their exact treatments, and `revoked` exposes **no** generation action anywhere in the Portal — no restore, no reactivate, no greyed control that hints at one (`EX-P15-08`).
- **AC-5** A capability the twin does not support renders as a plain sentence rather than an error (`capability_unsupported`, guard G-3), and voice preview uses the `P19.06` waveform player.
- **AC-6** Consent renders per §30.3 and §31.15: current version, scope with a **scroll-to-end gate** before the affirmative control enables, no pre-checked boxes, and the full version history. A superseded version requires re-acceptance.
- **AC-7** Revocation uses the destructive pattern (`P18.09` AC-6): typed confirmation, an explicit statement that generation stops immediately and that in-flight items move to `BLOCKED` (T22, G-1), no undo affordance because there is no undo, and — after revocation — the **propagation status** showing which downstream assets have been marked, because hiding the strongest guarantee in the product would make it invisible.

## BDD examples

- EX-P15-08 GIVEN a revoked Digital Twin, WHEN the twin-status screen renders, THEN it reports `revoked` and exposes no generation action.
- EX-P21-06 GIVEN a revoked Digital Twin, WHEN any Portal screen renders, THEN no generation action is exposed anywhere.
- EX-P21-28 GIVEN the consent scope has not been read to its end, WHEN the affirmative control is inspected, THEN it is disabled.
- EX-P21-29 GIVEN consent is revoked, WHEN the screen renders afterwards, THEN propagation status is shown and no undo control exists.

## Verification gate

Consumption folds from the ledger with reserved and committed distinguished and three threshold states; subscription states render with the payment absence stated as deliberate; the five twin states render and `revoked` exposes no generation action anywhere in the Portal; capability gaps render as sentences; the consent scroll-to-end gate blocks the affirmative; revocation requires typed confirmation, states the T22 consequence, offers no undo and shows propagation status.
