# Story P22.01 — Studio chrome, client scope and dense density

## User story

COMO operador, QUERO um escopo de cliente persistente e visível, PARA que eu nunca edite o roteiro do cliente errado.

## Context

`VYRA_PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE.md` §32.0 fixes the Studio shell: a persistent client-scope selector in the topbar that scopes every Production, Knowledge and Identity screen, while Distribution and Performance are cross-tenant by default. §22.1 commits Studio from `lg` upward and requires a designed refusal below `md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** The Studio route group renders the `P20` shell with Studio chrome: surface label, the **client-scope selector**, the command palette trigger, the notification bell and the account menu.
- **AC-2** The client scope is persistent, visible on every screen, reflected in the URL, and applied automatically to Production, Knowledge and Identity screens. Distribution and Performance default to cross-tenant and scope down on demand.
- **AC-3** With no client selected, cross-tenant screens work and client-scoped rail items are **disabled with the reason**, not hidden — an operator must be able to see that the capability exists and what it needs.
- **AC-4** Dense density is applied at the surface root via the `P18.04` token override, with zero component-level branching.
- **AC-5** An `Acting on behalf of {client}` banner in `status-warning` tone renders on every screen where the operator is acting for a tenant under `FR-S07`, and its presence is a precondition for those actions rather than a decoration.
- **AC-6** Below `md`, every Studio route renders the designed refusal from `P20.06` AC-3 with a link to the Portal where the session has access.

## BDD examples

- EX-P22-08 GIVEN Studio at 375px, WHEN any route renders, THEN the designed refusal appears and passes the conformance harness.
- EX-P22-09 GIVEN no client is selected, WHEN the rail renders, THEN client-scoped items are disabled with their reason rather than hidden.

## Verification gate

Studio chrome renders on every route; client scope persists, round-trips through the URL and applies to the correct screen families; the no-scope state disables with reasons; dense density is token-only; the acting-on-behalf banner renders wherever `FR-S07` applies; the sub-`md` refusal renders for every route.
