# Story P24.01 — Responsive conformance across the per-surface commitment matrix

## User story

COMO implementing agent, QUERO que o compromisso responsivo de cada superfície seja verificado em CI, PARA que 'funciona no celular' seja um teste e não uma afirmação.

## Context

`VYRA_PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE.md` §22.1 makes a **different** commitment per surface: Portal complete from `xs` to `3xl`, Studio and Control from `lg` with a degraded `md` and a designed refusal below. A single blanket responsive test would be wrong for two of the three surfaces; this story encodes the matrix.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** A commitment matrix is declared as data: per surface, per breakpoint, one of `full`, `degraded` or `refused`, exactly matching §22.1.
- **AC-2** Every screen renders at 320, 640, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1760 px and asserts, at each: no horizontal page scroll on the body, no clipped or unreachable interactive element, and no overlapping text.
- **AC-3** At a `full` breakpoint, every interactive element declared by the blueprint is present and operable. At a `degraded` breakpoint, the declared degradation is present **and stated to the user**. At a `refused` breakpoint, the designed refusal renders with its link to the Portal.
- **AC-4** Wide content — tables, boards, charts, code blocks — scrolls inside its own container with a visible edge affordance, never by scrolling the page (§8.4).
- **AC-5** Coarse-pointer emulation asserts ≥ 44×44 targets and the absence of hover-only affordances on every screen that commits to touch.
- **AC-6** The suite runs in `security/static` and fails naming the screen, the breakpoint and the violated assertion.

## BDD examples

- EX-P24-01 GIVEN the Portal at 320px with keyboard-only input, WHEN a user requests, approves and schedules content, THEN the whole journey completes.
- EX-P24-08 GIVEN Studio at 768px, WHEN a board renders, THEN the declared degradation is present and stated.

## Verification gate

The commitment matrix matches §22.1; every screen passes at all six viewports for its declared commitment level; wide content scrolls in-container; coarse-pointer assertions pass where touch is committed; a seeded horizontal page scroll fails naming screen and breakpoint.
