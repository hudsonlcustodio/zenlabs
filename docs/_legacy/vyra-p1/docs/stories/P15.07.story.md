## User story

COMO portal user, QUERO to watch the finished video and approve or reject it with a reason, PARA I control what goes out in my name.

## Context

This is the client approval screen that `epic-P12` and `P12.03` place out of their own scope. FR-P05 requires approval or rejection with a reason; T15 and T16 fix the transitions, and T15 cannot succeed without a passing human QA record regardless of tenant policy (FF-33).

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** The video is played through a short-TTL signed URL issued after an authorization check, never from a public object (`security-architecture.md` §6).
- **AC-2** Approve posts to the T15 sub-action with an `Idempotency-Key`; reject posts to T16 and requires a reason, per the T16 precondition.
- **AC-3** An item without a passing QA record offers no approval control and, if the route is called anyway, the server denies it — the screen never becomes the QA gate (FF-33).
- **AC-4** When the tenant policy is `AUTO`, the screen states that client acceptance was applied by policy and that VYRA QA still occurred, per FR-AP06.

## BDD examples

- EX-P15-02 GIVEN a screen that hides an action for a role, WHEN the underlying route is called directly, THEN the server denies it.
- EX-P15-05 GIVEN a media asset in the library, WHEN it is viewed, THEN delivery uses a short-TTL signed URL and never a public object.

## Verification gate

Class 3 contract coverage of T15 and T16 from the rendered screen; class 6 coverage of the portal-user restriction; FF-33 unaffected by any screen state.
