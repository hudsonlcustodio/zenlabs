## User story

COMO implementing agent, QUERO que a preferência por menos movimento seja verificada tela a tela, PARA que uma única animação esquecida não quebre um contrato de acessibilidade.

## Context

`VYRA_PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE.md` §10.4 fixes the reduced-motion contract and §10.1 the duration budget. `P18.06` AC-6 proves it for primitives; a screen can still introduce a transition of its own, and that is exactly what this story catches. §34 C-10 makes it a screen criterion.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, every screen is rendered and exercised through its state transitions, asserting no computed animation or transition duration exceeds 1ms except the sanctioned opacity fades at `dur-fast`.
- **AC-2** The specific substitutions of §10.4 are asserted per screen where they apply: `glow-live` static, skeleton shimmer flat, chart draw-in skipped, drag without fly animation but with its keyboard path intact.
- **AC-3** Without the preference, the motion budget is asserted: no duration exceeds 480ms except an indeterminate progress indicator, and no animated property is outside `transform` and `opacity`.
- **AC-4** No screen auto-advances anything — no carousel, no auto-rotating panel, no timed navigation — with or without the preference (§10.4).
- **AC-5** List reflow is asserted capped at 24 animated rows, beyond which animation is disabled (§10.5).

## BDD examples

- EX-P24-04 GIVEN `prefers-reduced-motion`, WHEN any screen renders and transitions, THEN no animation exceeds 1ms except the sanctioned opacity fades.
- EX-P24-13 GIVEN a screen with a 600ms transition, WHEN the motion-budget assertion runs, THEN it fails naming the screen and the property.

## Verification gate

Every screen passes the reduced-motion assertion across its state transitions; the §10.4 substitutions are asserted where applicable; the motion budget and animatable-property restriction hold without the preference; no screen auto-advances; list reflow caps at 24 rows.
