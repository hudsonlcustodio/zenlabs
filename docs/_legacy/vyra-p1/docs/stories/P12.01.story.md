## User story

COMO QA reviewer, QUERO my verdict recorded as a first-class fact, PARA the quality gate is auditable rather than implied by a state change.

## Context

FR-AP05 and ADR-0033: MVP video QA policy is `HUMAN_REQUIRED` and not tenant-configurable. T13 requires a QA record with an explicit human verdict.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** `qa_record` stores verdict, reviewer identity, reason and timestamp, tenant-scoped.
- **AC-2** A verdict is attributable to a specific human `QA_REVIEWER`; no system actor can author one.
- **AC-3** `QAPolicy` is modelled as extensible to `HUMAN_REQUIRED`, `AI_ASSISTED` and a future automated policy, with only `HUMAN_REQUIRED` active (FR-AP07, ASM-P12-01).
- **AC-4** The record is the object T13's precondition consults, not a boolean on the item.

## BDD examples

- EX-P12-02 GIVEN an actor who is not a `QA_REVIEWER`, WHEN they submit a QA verdict, THEN it is denied.

## Verification gate

Integration plus class 5 coverage of the T13 precondition.
