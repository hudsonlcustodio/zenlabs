# Story P5.02 — Identity owner distinct from portal user

- **Story ID**: `P5.02`
- **Epic**: `P5` (`docs/epics/epic-P5.md`)
- **Status**: `draft`
- **Wave**: 4
- **Priority**: P0
- **Story points**: 3
- **Depends on**: `P5.01`
- **IMPLEMENTATION NOT STARTED**

## User story

COMO governance owner, QUERO the consent subject modelled separately from the account holder, PARA consent, revocation and audit stay coherent if the roles ever diverge.

## Context

`prd.md` §3.1 states the identity owner is distinct from the portal user even when they are the same human. ASM-P03 records that MVP normally collapses them.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** `identity_owner` exists as its own entity holding consent, revocation and deletion rights.
- **AC-2** A portal user may reference an identity owner without being one.
- **AC-3** Rights exercisable only by the identity owner are guarded by the P3.06 role model rather than by the tenant membership alone.

## BDD examples

- EX-P5-02a GIVEN a portal user who is not the identity owner, WHEN they attempt to revoke consent, THEN the attempt is denied.

## Verification gate

Integration tests plus class 6 coverage of owner-only routes.
