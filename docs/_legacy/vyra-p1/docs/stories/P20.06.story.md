# Story P20.06 — Responsive shell across the breakpoint matrix

## User story

COMO usuário em qualquer dispositivo, QUERO que o shell se adapte de forma projetada e não degradada, PARA que o Portal funcione no celular e que Studio e Control recusem telas pequenas de forma honesta.

## Context

`VYRA_PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE.md` §22.1 makes a per-surface commitment: Portal is complete from `xs` to `3xl` because the client's one required act is an approval and approvals happen on phones; Studio and Control commit from `lg` and refuse below `md` with a designed state rather than a broken layout. §22.2 fixes the shell's four adaptations.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** The four shell adaptations of §22.2 are implemented: 248px rail at `≥ xl`; 64px icon rail expanding on hover **and on focus** at `lg`; off-canvas rail opened from a menu button at `md`; bottom tab bar of at most five labelled items below `md`.
- **AC-2** The Portal commitment holds from 320px upward: every Portal shell affordance is reachable and operable, the primary action docks to a bottom bar with safe-area padding, and nothing scrolls horizontally.
- **AC-3** Studio and Control render a **designed refusal** below `md` — a clear statement, the reason, and a link to the mobile-capable Portal where the session has access. It is a state, not a broken layout, and it is covered by the conformance harness like any other screen.
- **AC-4** The off-canvas rail and the bottom tab bar are fully keyboard operable, trap focus while open where modal, and return focus on close.
- **AC-5** Coarse pointers get ≥ 44×44 targets throughout the shell, no hover-only affordance, and a long-press equivalent for every context menu (§22.4).
- **AC-6** A breakpoint traversal test renders the shell for all three surfaces at 320, 640, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1760 px, asserting no horizontal page scroll and no unreachable affordance at any of them.

## BDD examples

- EX-P20-05 GIVEN a viewport of 320px, WHEN the shell renders, THEN navigation is a bottom tab bar of at most five labelled items and nothing scrolls horizontally.
- EX-P20-14 GIVEN Studio at 375px, WHEN it renders, THEN a designed refusal state appears with a link to the Portal.
- EX-P20-15 GIVEN the icon rail at `lg`, WHEN a keyboard user focuses it, THEN it expands as it would on hover.

## Verification gate

The four adaptations render at their breakpoints; the Portal is operable at 320px with a docked primary action; Studio and Control render a designed refusal below `md`; the off-canvas rail and tab bar are keyboard operable with correct focus return; coarse-pointer targets meet 44×44; the six-viewport traversal passes for all three surfaces.
