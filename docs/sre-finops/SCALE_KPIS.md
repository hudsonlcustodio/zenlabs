# SCALE KPIs

## Straight-Through Production Rate
`productions reaching READY without human intervention / total productions`

## Human Touch Rate
`productions touched by a human / total productions`

## Exception Rate
Track both:
- production exception rate;
- shot exception rate.

## Human Minutes per Final Hour
`human intervention minutes / final approved hours`

## Cost per Approved Minute
`all attributable provider/processing cost / final approved minutes`

## First-Pass Acceptance
`shots or productions passing QC on first attempt / total`

## Auto-Repair Success
`exceptions repaired automatically / repairable exceptions`

## Pod capacity
Do not forecast by client count alone. Combine volume, production count, shot count, pack complexity, risk and exception rate.
